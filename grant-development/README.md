# Grant Development

This directory holds **public-safe** grant-development material that is distinct from the underlying historical research.

> **Repository classification:** `historical-research-project` is a public repository. Nothing placed here should depend on confidentiality or trade-secret protection. Detailed non-public proposals, budgets, partner negotiations, unpublished commercial strategy, personal/contact information, NDA-covered material, or proprietary RPG design belong in the private project workspace under the LEG-001 disclosure protocol.

Appropriate public-safe contents may include:

- publicly announced grant calls and deadlines;
- public eligibility criteria and programme notes;
- public-facing concept summaries intentionally approved for release;
- high-level work-package structures that reveal no confidential commercial information;
- published/open-data/licensing/IP requirements of funding programmes;
- public go/no-go rationale where deliberate publication creates no project risk.

Keep the following **out of this public repository unless intentionally approved for publication**:

- detailed proposal drafts containing unpublished project strategy;
- detailed budgets, runway assumptions or private financial models;
- partner/host negotiation notes and non-public contact information;
- confidential letters, agreements or reviewer/adviser correspondence;
- unpublished RPG narrative, mechanics or production architecture;
- material subject to an NDA, institutional confidentiality requirement or third-party restriction.

A grant call should be selected only after the research question, scholarly contribution, host/PI model, proposed outputs and disclosure implications are clear. When in doubt, develop the material in the private project repository first and publish only a sanitised, intentional public version.
